    How to jam

    1. Pick a layer: Space switches between Groove, Lead and Extra.
    2. Pick a song: hold the mouse button, drag through the songs and let go on the one you want (or just click it). It drops in on the next bar, synced to the tempo and key. Songs without a stem for the current layer show as empty slots.
    3. Mix songs: fill each layer from a different track. A / D flip albums.
    4. Pick an Audio FX (stutter, low-pass, high-pass, phaser, wah or tape stop) under the Audio FX button, then hold F to play it. Let go and it snaps back.

    Enter Jam (top left, or click empty space) hides the cursor so small mouse flicks steer the wheel. Tab exits.
