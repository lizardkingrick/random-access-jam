    How to jam

    1. Pick a layer in the middle of the wheel — Groove, Lead or Extra.
    2. Pick a song on the wheel. It drops in on the next bar, synced to the tempo and key.
    3. Mix songs — fill each layer from a different track. Flip albums with the tiles under the wheel.
    4. Hold Audio FX and move to sweep a filter and echo.
